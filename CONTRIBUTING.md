# Contributing

The Lexicon Protocol is an early-stage research project. Contributions that improve falsifiability, reproducibility, safety, transparency, and technical rigor are especially welcome.

## Useful contribution areas

- controlled natural language and formal methods;
- schema and constraint validation;
- AI safety and adversarial evaluation;
- benchmark and rubric design;
- uncertainty and calibration;
- provenance and auditability;
- accessible user interfaces;
- documentation and reproducible experiments.

## Contribution principles

1. Keep human authority explicit; do not introduce autonomous binding decision paths.
2. Separate deterministic findings from model-generated findings.
3. Document assumptions and trust boundaries.
4. Add tests or benchmark cases for new validation behavior where practical.
5. Avoid proprietary, personal, classified, security-sensitive, or otherwise restricted data.
6. Prefer reproducible prompts, structured outputs, and versioned schemas.

## Process

Open an issue describing the problem or proposed experiment before large changes. Pull requests should explain the motivation, assumptions, validation approach, and any new model/API dependency.

By contributing, you agree that your contributions will be licensed under the repository's MIT License.
